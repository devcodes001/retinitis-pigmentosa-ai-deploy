ℹ️ About RPeyecare

How It Works

A complete end-to-end clinical AI platform built for early detection of Retinitis Pigmentosa.

1

Upload a Retinal Fundus Image

Patient uploads a color fundus photograph or selects one of the pre-loaded benchmark samples. Accepted formats: PNG, JPG, BMP (max 20 MB).

2

ResNet-50 Deep Feature Extraction

The image is pre-processed (contrast enhancement + sharpening) and fed into a fine-tuned ResNet-50 model trained on labelled RP retinal datasets. Softmax probabilities are computed for both classes.

3

Risk Stratification & Report

Results are categorized as LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH risk. A clinical PDF report is instantly generated with patient details, probability breakdown, and clinical recommendations — downloadable in one click.

4

Clinic Referral & Booking

High-risk patients are directed to book an in-person consultation with a verified ophthalmology clinic. The clinic portal manages the appointment queue with real-time status updates.
